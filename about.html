About

From applied mathematics to the atmosphere.

I’m a PhD researcher at the Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse, working on explainable AI for data-driven weather forecasting with colleagues at Météo-France and Cerfacs. The question behind it sounds simple: when a deep learning model forecasts the weather, can we understand why, and is its reasoning physically plausible?

Before the PhD I studied computer science and applied mathematics at Grenoble INP – Ensimag, with an exchange semester in machine learning at KTH in Stockholm. Along the way I worked on physics-informed machine learning for cardiac modelling at Dassault Systèmes, and on neural operators for the Kuramoto–Sivashinsky equation at the Laboratoire Hubert Curien.
