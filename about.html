About

From applied mathematics to the atmosphere.

I’m a PhD researcher at the Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse, working on explainable AI for data-driven weather forecasting with colleagues at Météo-France and Cerfacs. The question sounds simple: when a deep learning model forecasts the weather, can we understand why, and is its reasoning physically plausible?

Before that I studied computer science and applied mathematics at Grenoble INP – Ensimag, with an exchange semester in machine learning at KTH in Stockholm.
