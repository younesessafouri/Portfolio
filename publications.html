Preprint Under review

Explanation of Dynamic Physical Field Predictions using WassersteinGrad

Essafouri, Y., Raynaud, L., Drozda, L., Risser, L.
arXiv:2604.22580 · 2026

Perturbing the input of a weather model does not make its explanation noisier — it makes it move. We show that stochastic perturbations geometrically displace gradient attributions on atmospheric fields, then replace pointwise averaging with an entropic Wasserstein barycenter to recover a spatially coherent explanation.

Conference Poster

A Framework for Explainable AI in Weather Forecasting: Diagnosing Deep Learning Models via Gradient-Based Attributions

Essafouri, Y., Seznec, C., Drozda, L., Raynaud, L., Risser, L.
EGU General Assembly 2026, Vienna · EGU26-4039

A four-step framework for producing localised explanations of neural weather forecasts: choose an input field, an output variable, and a region of interest, then read the attribution back as a map. Presented to bridge machine-learning explainability and operational meteorology.
