Research

Reading neural weather forecasts backwards.

Data-driven models now match numerical weather prediction on many scores, at a fraction of the cost, but they don’t say why. When one predicts heavy rain, a forecaster cannot ask what in the atmosphere made it say so.

I build attribution methods that trace a forecast back to the input fields behind it, and test whether the answer is faithful to the model, stable under small perturbations, and physically plausible.
