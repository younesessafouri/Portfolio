Research

Can a weather model's reasoning survive a second opinion?

Deep learning forecasters now rival or beat classical numerical weather prediction on many metrics — but their internal reasoning is opaque. My PhD builds attribution methods that trace a forecast back to the input fields that produced it, so operational meteorologists can tell physically grounded reasoning from spurious correlation.

Explaining Neural Weather Models

PhD research — Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse

A framework that interrogates deep forecasting models by tracing the influence of input fields — geopotential, humidity, wind — onto the variables they predict. The goal: separate real physical reasoning from spurious correlation, and make the result legible to an operational meteorologist.
