  1. NeurIPS 2026

    Explanation of Dynamic Physical Field Predictions using WassersteinGrad: Application to Autoregressive Weather Forecasting

    Younes Essafouri, Laure Raynaud, Luciano Drozda, Laurent Risser

    Paper Project

    Perturbing the input of a neural weather model does not make its gradient explanation noisier; it makes it move. We show that stochastic input perturbations geometrically displace attribution maps on dynamic physical fields, an effect that compounds under autoregressive rollout, and replace SmoothGrad’s pointwise averaging with an entropic Wasserstein barycenter that recovers a spatially coherent geometric consensus.

About

Neural networks now forecast the weather about as well as the physics-based models they learned from. I try to understand how they use physical information: which parts of the atmosphere a forecast relies on, how that influence travels from one forecast step to the next, and whether it makes physical sense.

PhD in explainable AI for weather forecasting at the Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse, since 2025, with researchers at Météo-France and Cerfacs and support from ANITI. Before that, applied mathematics and scientific machine learning.

Off the clock I take photographs, read the novel before the film, and play records that never left the twentieth century.
